Fraud decisions that ask the witness.

When the evidence on a payment is split, most fraud systems guess. Subpoena.ai convenes a courtroom of AI agents that argue the case from evidence, and when they deadlock, it asks the cardholder directly over WhatsApp, SMS or a phone call, verifies it's really them, and rules with their answer.

A blocked card at the checkout is a customer who calls support, or leaves. A missed fraud is a chargeback. The hard cases sit in the middle, where the data alone can't tell, and the one person who knows the answer is never asked.

How it works

From payment to verdict, in four steps.

1

Screen

Hard rules and a risk score clear the obvious cases in under a millisecond. Only the unclear ones go to court.

2

Debate

The Clerk and five expert agents gather evidence. Prosecutor and Defense argue; any claim without a cited finding is struck before the Judge sees it.

3

Subpoena

On a deadlock, the Bailiff checks for a SIM swap and the cardholder gets one question and a one-time code by WhatsApp, SMS or a phone call.

4

Verdict

The Judge re-rules with the verified testimony. The bank gets a signed webhook; the Stenographer writes the plain-English why.

The courtroom

Every agent has one job, and only the tools for it.

Each agent connects to its own MCP endpoint, so it can only reach what its role needs. The Prosecutor can't message a customer; the Witness can't see the evidence.

Clerk

Opens the case and pulls baselines, history and device data.

Expert witnesses

Geo, Device, Velocity, Merchant and Graph each file cited findings.

Prosecutor

Argues for blocking, from the findings only.

Defense

Argues for approving, and rebuts the prosecution.

Judge

Rules a calibrated p_fraud and, if needed, issues the subpoena.

Bailiff

SIM-swap lookup and one-time code before any testimony counts.

Stenographer

Explains the verdict in plain English for analysts and customers.

Analyst

The human appeals court: override any verdict from the Console.

The witness

Ask the customer where they already are.

The subpoena goes out on the first channel the bank's policy allows. If it isn't delivered, because the WhatsApp window has closed or a call isn't answered, it moves to the next.

💬

WhatsApp

Question, code and a photo of the receipt in one chat.

✉️

SMS

Two-way text from the bank's number.

📞

Phone call

The question is read aloud; the code goes on the keypad and the answer is spoken.

🧾

Receipt photos

Read by Gemini and checked against the payment. A receipt that doesn't match never auto-approves.

Built for a regulated room

Guardrails are in the architecture, not the prompt.

Testimony is untrusted

A tool-less extractor turns the customer's words into structured claims. "Ignore previous instructions" is just text.

Identity before testimony

Carrier SIM-swap check, then a one-time code. Unknown SIM status fails closed.

Cite or be struck

Arguments that don't reference a finding never reach the Judge.

Policy decides, not the model

The Judge outputs a probability; the bank's versioned policy maps it to an outcome.

Every change is on record

Append-only audit log, analyst overrides with reasons, signed webhooks.

Least privilege for people

Google sign-in, Admin / Analyst / Viewer roles, personal data masked for viewers.

Developers

Send the transaction; clear cases come back in the response. Courtroom cases return 202 and stream every step over server-sent events, then post the verdict to your webhook, Slack, Pub/Sub or BigQuery.
